# Niche Hunt

> **Task · หา niche กลุ่มเป้าหมายเฉพาะ** — the goal of this step. Uses `RECON.md` (who pays) + `WORLD-ECOSYSTEMS.md` (platforms/business of regions) to lock onto ONE specific, reachable niche.

## The task
Pick **one** niche where I can find a **real group** that:
1. **Pays** for what I can do (geospatial × AI, CV, "proves the analysis").
2. Is **reachable** by a solo student (English, LinkedIn/X, ~0 cost).
3. Has a clear **recurring pain** I can solve better than the giant players.
4. I **genuinely want** to keep working on (because I'll be at it for months).

## How to do it (from the recon)
1. **Draw down from 4 patterns** (from WORLD-ECOSYSTEMS.md): money buys proof · open-infra = new surface · build on top of big platform · my edge = geospatial×weather×AI.
2. **Scan this shortlist** and rank by effort-to-first-client and pay:
   - Gulf / MEA — climate/energy/geospatial risk data (insurers, energy, ag)
   - India / SEA — agritech, flood/weather, SME digitization, open-rails (UPI/ONDC) AI layer
   - US / EU — AI/ML automation tools for a vertical (data, climate, CV)
3. **Validate the top pick cheaply**: talk to 5–10 people in that group (listen, don't sell); confirm they **already pay** someone for this.
4. **Commit the niche** in OFFER.md and move to Phase A (positioning) / B (offer).

## DEFINITION OF DONE — what "nit works" looks like ✅
When this task **works**, I will have:
- **[1] A named, specific niche** — one sentence: *"I serve [this specific group of people] who need [this one outcome], and they're in [this place/industry]."* (Not "everyone who needs AI.")
- **[2] Evidence they pay** — proof that this group **already spends money** on the problem (a tool/consultant/employee they pay for today).
- **[3] A reachable channel** — the concrete place I can meet them (e.g., LinkedIn group, a subreddit, Upwork category, a Slack/Discord community, a trade conference) and the English around it.
- **[4] 3 ideal-customer names** — 3 real, specific organizations/people who would buy; who I can message.
- **[5] A validated 1-line offer** — the exact wording that gets a "yes, that's my problem" reaction (feeds Phase B).

## When this DONE, the business looks like
- I stop guessing. I have a **repeatable profile** of exactly who to talk to.
- Every hour goes to the **right** people (not cold strangers). First client is much closer.
- The niche becomes the **seed of the later product/platform** — the exact group whose pain the platform will solve.
