# Research

The thread that runs through everything I do: **turning space + weather data into provable insights.**

## Focus

- **Spatio-temporal Geospatial Foundation Models (GFM)** — models that learn the behavior of places over time.
- **Satellite × weather fusion** — combining remote sensing with meteorological data.
- **Zero-shot computer vision** (SAM3) — segmenting and judging what's in an image without per-class training.

## Why this matters (north star)

I want to build systems that don't just output a number, but **prove the analysis** — so people (and businesses) can trust the answer. That's the "wisdom" I'm building toward. It's what separates my work from a simple map.
